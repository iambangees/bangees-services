Questions, answered

How fast will my website be ready?

A Launch site takes 2–3 days, a Business site 5–7 days, once your deposit and your content (logo, photos, prices) are in. You will get a delivery date in writing before we start.

What do I need to provide?

Your business name, logo if you have one, photos, your services and prices, and your WhatsApp number. If your wording needs polish, that is included. Your prices and facts always come from you.

Are there any yearly costs?

Only your domain name, roughly ₦10,000–₦30,000 per year, registered in your name so you own it. Hosting is free, and the optional Care Plan is ₦15,000 per month if you want updates done for you.

Can I update the site myself?

On custom builds, yes, an admin panel can be included. On Launch and Business sites, most clients prefer the Care Plan: you send a WhatsApp message and the change is made for you.

I already have Instagram. Why do I need a website?

Instagram rents you an audience that can disappear with one hacked or suspended account. A website is the one place online you own, it shows up on Google when people search, and it makes your Instagram page convert better, because serious customers can check your prices and reach your WhatsApp in one tap.

How do I pay?

50% deposit by transfer to start, and the balance on launch day when your site is live. You never pay the full amount upfront.
